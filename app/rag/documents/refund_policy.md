# Refund Policy

Customers are eligible for a full refund within 30 days of purchase if the order status is "completed".
Orders that have already been refunded or cancelled cannot be refunded again.

Refunds under $100 are processed automatically without manual review.
Refunds of $100 or more require approval from a support supervisor before being issued.

Refund processing typically takes 3-5 business days to appear on the original payment method.
