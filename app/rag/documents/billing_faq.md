# Billing FAQ

Q: Why was I charged twice?
A: Duplicate charges are usually caused by a failed payment retry. Check your invoice history --
if two invoices show the same amount on the same day, contact support for a reversal.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept major credit cards, debit cards, and PayPal.

Q: How do I update my billing information?
A: Billing details can be updated from your account settings page under "Payment Methods".

Q: My invoice shows "unpaid" but I already paid. What do I do?
A: Payment processing can take up to 24 hours to reflect. If it's still unpaid after 24 hours,
contact support with your payment confirmation number.
