# Account Help

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the emailed reset link.

Q: How do I delete my account?
A: Account deletion requests must be submitted via support@opspilot.example.com.
Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone after 30 days.

Q: Can I have multiple accounts under one email?
A: No, each email address can only be associated with one account.
